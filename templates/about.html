Who We Are Singubani Thina

Nogidela Holdings is a professional service provider based in Centane in the Eastern Cape, dedicated to delivering quality services to our community. From grass cutting and tree felling to meat processing and equipment hiring, we bring expertise, reliability, and care to every project. I-Nogidela Holdings ngumnikezeli weenkonzo zobugcisa esekelwe kuCentane eMpuma Koloni, ezinikele ekunikezeleni iinkonzo ezisemgangathweni kuluntu lwethu. Ukusuka ekusikolweni kwengca nokugawula imithi ukuya ekuqhawuleni inyama nasekuqesheni izixhobo, sizisa ubuchule, ukuthembeka, nenkathalo kwiprojekthi nganye.

We understand the needs of our community and are committed to providing services that make a real difference in people's lives, whether for personal events, agricultural needs, or business projects. Siyaqonda iimfuno zoluntu lwethu kwaye sizibophelele ekunikezeleni iinkonzo ezenza umahluko wokwenene ebomini babantu, nokuba yimicimbi yobuqu, iimfuno zezolimo, okanye iiprojekthi zeshishini.
